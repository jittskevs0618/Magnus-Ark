## 5. Services

**Heading:** What we do

**Lead:**

> Four capabilities, run by one company. Platforms we support do not have to assemble them from four vendors.

### Platform technology

> We design, build, and administer the gaming systems that licensed platforms run on. This covers core platform architecture, content and payment integrations, system monitoring, release engineering, and the reporting infrastructure that regulatory oversight depends on.

### Operations

> Customer support, account verification, payments processing, and integrity monitoring, staffed and supervised in-house. Our operations teams work to documented procedures with defined escalation paths, and every action against a customer account is logged and reviewable.

### Marketing

> Brand development, performance marketing, lifecycle and retention programmes, and creative production. All campaign material passes internal compliance review before it runs, so advertising claims stay inside what the regulator permits.

### Compliance and player protection

> Regulatory reporting, anti-money-laundering procedures, age and identity verification, and responsible play controls. This function reports independently of commercial targets, because a compliance team that answers to revenue is not a compliance team.

## 6. Standards

**Heading:** How we work

Four items. Short, no headings inside them — these read as a list of commitments.

> **We hold the license, so we hold the responsibility.** Regulatory obligations sit with us directly. They are not delegated to a vendor and they are not treated as somebody else's paperwork.

> **Player protection is a design constraint.** Age verification, spend controls, self-exclusion, and activity monitoring are built into the platform layer. They cannot be switched off for a promotion.

> **Data stays where it should.** Customer records are held under Philippine data privacy law with access controls, retention limits, and audit logging on every read.

> **We hire Filipino and we train deep.** The specialist skills this sector needs — platform engineering, payments integrity, regulatory operations — are built here rather than imported.
